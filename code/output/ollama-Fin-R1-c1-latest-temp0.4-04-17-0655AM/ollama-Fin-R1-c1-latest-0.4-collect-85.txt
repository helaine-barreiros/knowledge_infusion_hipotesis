**Key Observations and Explanations:**
- **Client Actors**: The Client is the primary user interacting with various features like viewing dashboards, receiving recommendations, managing alerts, etc. Each client-related use case (CFR) has a direct relationship to this actor.
  
- **Financial Advisor Actors**: Financial Advisors have their own set of interactions related to mentoring clients and reviewing system-generated insights. These are represented under the "FAFR" namespace.

- **System Administrator Actor**: The SysAdmin manages AI parameters for compliance, such as Basel III or financial regulations (SAFR001), and maintains historical records (SFR020).

- **Use Case Grouping**:
  - Clients: CFRs focus on viewing data, receiving alerts, managing preferences.
  - Advisors: FAFRs involve mentoring clients, reviewing alerts/predictions.
  - SysAdmin: SAFR for compliance management.

This structure ensures clarity in distinguishing between client interactions and system administration/financial advisory roles. Each use case is linked to the appropriate actor based on their functional requirements.
