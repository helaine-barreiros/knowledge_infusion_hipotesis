5. **Client Management System:**
   - Manages client relationships, tracks financial goals progress (`SFR018`), and handles user feedback (`CFR011`). This ensures that the system remains responsive to client needs and regulatory requirements.

6. **Data Processing System:**

7. **Contextualization System:**

8. **Feedback System:**
