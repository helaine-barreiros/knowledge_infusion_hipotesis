### Explanation:
- **Client**: Can view financial insights dashboards and receive various alerts. They also manage their recommendations preferences.
- **Financial Advisor**: Manages client mentoring sessions, views predictive analytics about clients' financial situations, and reviews system-generated intervention alerts.
- **System Administrator**: Configures analysis parameters for the AI to ensure regulatory compliance (e.g., Basel III).
- **AI Component**: Generates predictions, personalized recommendations, spending limit alerts, investment opportunity analyses, anomaly transaction alerts, contextualized news insights, etc.

### Key Relationships:
1. **Client** interacts with multiple use cases directly.
2. **Financial Advisor** and **System Administrator** have specific interactions tied to their roles (e.g., FAFR004 for advisors vs. SAFR001/SFR005 for admins).
3. The system itself requests data from the AI component via SFR003, which drives other use cases like AIFR004.

This structure ensures clarity in role-specific interactions and dependencies within the system. Adjustments can be made if additional relationships or actors are required based on further analysis of requirements.
