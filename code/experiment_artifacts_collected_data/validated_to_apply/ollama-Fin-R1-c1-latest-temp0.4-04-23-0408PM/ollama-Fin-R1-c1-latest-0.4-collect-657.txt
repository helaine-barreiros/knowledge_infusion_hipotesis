**Key Observations and Grouping:**
- **Client Use Cases:** All use cases related to the client’s interaction with the system, such as viewing dashboards, receiving alerts, managing preferences, etc.
- **Financial Advisor Use Cases:** Activities specific to advisors, like mentoring sessions, predictive analytics for clients, generating recommendations, etc.
- **System Administrator Use Cases:** Management of analysis parameters and system settings (e.g., SAFR001).
- **System/Component Interactions:** Represented by the "SFR" use cases which involve requests from the client to the AI or other components within the system.

**Note:**
The `User` actor is used here as a general term encompassing clients, financial advisors, and administrators where applicable. The diagram groups related use cases under their respective actors for clarity.
