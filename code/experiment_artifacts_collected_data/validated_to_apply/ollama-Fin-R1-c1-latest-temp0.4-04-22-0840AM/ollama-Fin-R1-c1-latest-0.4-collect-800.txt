**Detailed Explanation of the Diagram Structure:**

### System Boundary:
- The entire diagram is enclosed within a rectangle labeled **"System,"** which represents the scope of interactions for all actors and use cases.

### Client Role:
- **Client (c)** interacts with multiple use cases, including viewing financial insights, managing alerts/preferences, receiving recommendations, customizing preferences, monitoring spending limits, viewing investment opportunities, accessing consolidated dashboards, and managing relationships with advisors.
  
  - **CFR001: View Financial Insights Dashboard** – Client requests a comprehensive view of their financial data to understand overall health.
  - **SAFR002: Manage Alert Preferences** – Adjusts alert settings for transactions or spending limits.
  - **CFR003: View Financial Predictions** – Sees future projections based on historical and market trends.
  - **CFR004: Receive Personalized Recommendations** – Gets tailored financial advice aligned with their profile.
  - **CFR005: Customize Recommendation Preferences** – Adjusts the system’s recommendation parameters (e.g., risk tolerance).
  - **CFR006: Monitor Spending Limits** – Tracks spending against predefined thresholds for budget control.
  - **SAFR003: View Investment Opportunities** – Identifies investment options matching their risk profile and goals.
  - **CFR012: Access Consolidated Financial Dashboard** – A single view of all financial information.
  - **CFR014: Manage Relationship with Advisors** – Controls data sharing permissions for advisors.

### Financial Advisor Role:
- **FinancialAdvisor (fa)** handles client interactions, including mentoring sessions, alerts, predictive analytics, and parameter configuration by segment.
  
  - **FAFR001: Manage Client Mentoring Sessions** – Plans and tracks advisory meetings based on system-generated insights.
  - **FAFR002: View Intervention Alerts** – Receives notifications for clients needing immediate attention (e.g., unusual transactions).
  - **FAFR003: View Predictive Analytics for Clients** – Analyzes client financial data to provide guidance during consultations.
  - **FAFR004: Configure Analysis Parameters by Segment** – Customizes system behavior based on specific client profiles or regulatory requirements.

### System Administrator Role:
- **SysAdmin (sa)** focuses solely on managing analysis parameters across the entire system, ensuring compliance and accuracy in algorithms.
  
  - **SAFR001: Manage Analysis Parameters** – Adjusts global settings for all AI components to maintain relevance and regulation adherence.
