**Key Observations:**
- **Client Interface:** Primary interaction points include viewing dashboards, receiving alerts, making predictions, getting recommendations, managing preferences, etc.
- **Alert Management System (SFR013):** Handles system-generated alerts based on client-specific conditions or thresholds set by SysAdmins or the AI component. Alerts can be for financial health, compliance issues like AML/FATF, spending limits, investment opportunities, and more.
- **Recommendation Engine:** Central to personalized services, this subsystem uses historical data, market trends, risk profiles, etc., to generate tailored recommendations (e.g., investments, savings plans). It also interfaces with the AI components for real-time analysis.
- **Investment Opportunity Analysis:** Focuses on aligning investment opportunities with user’s risk profile and ESG preferences. This involves evaluating financial instruments against Modern Portfolio Theory principles and other regulatory standards.
- **News Service Integration (AIFR007):** Extracts relevant news from sources to contextualize portfolio impacts, ensuring clients stay informed about market events affecting their assets.
- **Spending Limit Alert System:** Monitors transaction patterns for unusual activity. When spending exceeds predefined thresholds in specific categories, alerts are generated to help users maintain budget control and comply with financial regulations like Basel III (for risk management).
- **Client-Dashboard Generation (SFR012):** Consolidates all financial information into a single view, making it easier for clients to monitor their finances across various accounts or segments.
- **Client-Advisor Relationship Management:** Tracks progress towards financial goals and allows users to share data with advisors while managing permissions. This ensures privacy and control over sensitive information.
- **AI-Generated Predictions/Interventions (AIFR001):** Provides forward-looking analysis, such as future cash flow projections or market trend forecasts, which can inform strategic decisions like retirement planning or estate management.
- **Feedback Loop:** Encourages continuous improvement by allowing clients to report on system outputs. This data is used to refine algorithms and enhance the relevance of subsequent recommendations.
