### Explanation:
- **Client (User)** actors include all functional requirements related to viewing dashboards, receiving alerts, making predictions, managing recommendations, and tracking goals. These are grouped under the "Professional" rectangle.
- The system itself is represented as a boundary with internal use cases like `CFR019` for maintaining historical records.
- **Financial Advisor** actors handle client intervention alerts (`FAFR002`) and configuring analysis parameters (`FAFR004`). They also access trends from the historical record to analyze clients' financial behaviors.
- The **System Administrator** focuses on managing system-level use cases like `SAFR001` for compliance parameter management.

This structure ensures all actors, their interactions with specific use cases, and logical groupings are accurately represented. Adjustments can be made if additional relationships or inheritance need clarification based on the requirements.
