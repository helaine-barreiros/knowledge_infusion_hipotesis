**Key Explanations:**
- **Client**: Can view financial dashboards, alerts, and recommendations. They can also manage their relationship with Financial Advisors.
- **Financial Advisor (FA)**: Manages client mentoring sessions, views intervention alerts, and requests contextualized news insights or predictive analyses from the AI system.
- **System Administrator (SysAdmin)**: Manages analysis parameters for the AI system to ensure compliance with regulations like Basel III.
  
**Use Cases Grouping by Actor/Functionality:**
1. **Client**: CFR001–CFR004, FAFR002, SAFR002
2. **Financial Advisor (FA)**: FAFR001–FAFR003, AIFR007–AIFR009
3. **System Administrator**: SAFR001

**Notes on Inheritance and Relationships:**
- The Financial Advisor actor extends the Client role in some interactions but maintains distinct use cases.
- System components (e.g., AI systems) are represented as usecases within their respective packages, ensuring clarity.

This structure ensures all requirements are mapped accurately to actors and use cases while adhering to PlantUML syntax rules.
