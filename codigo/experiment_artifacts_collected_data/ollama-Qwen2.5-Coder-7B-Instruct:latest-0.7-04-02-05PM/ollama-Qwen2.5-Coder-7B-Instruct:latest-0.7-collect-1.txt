 The states include: 
1. Ready
2. Triage Initiated
3. Waiting for Triage
4. Triage Completed
5. Discharge Initiated
6. Discharge Completed
7. Finalized

The transitions between states are as follows:
1. From Ready to Triage Initiated when a patient is ready to be triaged.
2. From Triage Initiated to Waiting for Triage when the triage process starts.
3. From Waiting for Triage to Triage Completed when the triage process is completed.
4. From Triage Completed to Discharge Initiated when the discharge process is initiated.
5. From Discharge Initiated to Discharge Completed when the discharge process is completed.
6. From Discharge Completed to Finalized when the prontuary status is finalized.
