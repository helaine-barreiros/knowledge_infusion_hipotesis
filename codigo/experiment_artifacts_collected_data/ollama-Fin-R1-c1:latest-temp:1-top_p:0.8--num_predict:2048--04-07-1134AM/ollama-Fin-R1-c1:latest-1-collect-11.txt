### Explanation of the Diagram:
- **Authentication**: Users can authenticate using standard methods (username/password) or OAuth2 providers. This includes optional multi-factor authentication.
- **Financial Account Management**: Connects external financial accounts via banking APIs, views transactions, and categorizes them into expense categories.
- **Analysis & Insights**: Provides a financial dashboard with basic reports and advanced AI-driven insights for spending habits and recommendations. The latter can be extended to include chatbot interactions or other forms of personalization.
- **Planning**: Helps users set and track financial goals over time. Advisors are involved in tracking progress, which may involve more detailed analysis provided by the system.
- **Collaboration**: Allows sharing selected data with advisors for professional oversight.
