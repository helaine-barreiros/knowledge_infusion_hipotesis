This document defines the rules, constraints, and policies governing reservations, cancellations, payments, and guest interactions.

1. General Reservation Policies
1.1 Reservation Types
Standard Reservation: Requires payment within 24 hours of confirmation.    
NonRefundable Reservation: Discounted rate with no cancellation/refund eligibility.    
VIP/Corporate Reservation: Follows custom rules (see Section 5).    

1.2 Reservation Validity
Reservations expire if:    
Pending Confirmation: No action for 1 hour.
Awaiting Payment: No payment within 24 hours (standard) or 72 hours (VIP).
NoShow: Checkin missed by 24 hours postarrival date.

2. Cancellation Policies
2.1 GuestInitiated Cancellation
Timing:>48h before checkin;Refund Amount: 100% refund;Notes:Full refund processed within 5 business days.
Timing:24–48h before checkin; Refund Amount:50% refund;Notes:Partial refund; cancellation fee = 50% of booking total.
Timing:<24h before checkin; Refund Amount:0%; refund;Notes:Exceptions: Hotelinitiated cancellations (e.g., maintenance, overbooking).
Timing:NonRefundable Booking; Refund Amount:0% refund;Notes:Applies regardless of timing.

2.2 HotelInitiated Cancellation
Full refund issued for:
Overbooking: Unable to reassign room/partner hotel.     
Maintenance Hold: Room unavailable due to repairs.
Policy Violation: Hotel cancels due to fraud or invalid guest details.

3. Payment Policies
3.1 Payment Deadlines
Reservation Type:Standard;Payment Window:24 hours;Retry Attempts:2 retries
Reservation Type:NonRefundable;Payment Window:24 hours;Retry Attempts:0 retries
Reservation Type:VIP/Corporate;Payment Window:72 hours;Retry Attempts:3 retries

3.2 Payment Failure Handling
Failed Payments:
System retries according to reservation type (see table above).
After final failure: Reservation transitions to Cancelled.
Refunds for Payment Errors:
Automatically processed if payment succeeded but reservation invalid (e.g., overbooking).

4. Dynamic Pricing Policy
4.1 Price Adjustment Rules
Base Price: Determined by room type, season, and demand.
Extensions:
New price = (Original daily rate × remaining days) + (Current daily rate × extended days).
Peak season surcharge: +10% for extensions during highdemand periods.
Discounts:
EarlyBird: 10% off for bookings made >30 days in advance.
LastMinute: 15% off for bookings made <24h before checkin.

4.2 Price Lock Guarantee
Once payment is received, prices are locked and immune to dynamic changes.
    
5. Overbooking Policies
5.1 Overbooking Resolution
Prioritization Hierarchy:
1. Loyalty Program Members (highest tier first).    
2. Early Bookings (reservations made >60 days in advance).
3. Higher Room Tiers (suites prioritized over standard rooms).    

Compensation Options:  
Upgrade: Free room upgrade (if available).   
Partner Hotel: Relocation to prenegotiated partner property.
Compensation Package: 20% refund + 15% discount on future stay.

5.2 Guest Response Deadline
Guests must respond to overbooking resolution offers within 6 hours.  
No response → Reservation transitions to Cancelled with full refund.

6. VIP/Corporate Policies
6.1 Key Exceptions

Policy:Payment Window;VIP/Corporate Rule:72 hours;Standard Rule:24 hours

6.2 Dedicated Support
Concierge Service: VIP guests receive direct staff assistance for modifications.
Priority Notifications: Alerts sent 12h before deadlines (vs. 1h for standard).

7. NoShow & CheckIn Policies
7.1 NoShow Penalties
Standard Guests: Reservation forfeited; no refund.   
VIP Guests: 25% refund if checkin missed.
Automatic Transition: Reservation moves to Expired after 24h.
 
7.2 CheckIn Window
Guests must check in by 2:00 PM on arrival date.
Late checkin: Room held until 12:00 PM the following day.

8. Extension Policies
8.1 Extension Request Rules
Eligibility: Only allowed during InUse state.
Availability Check: System verifies room availability for new dates.
Price Adjustment: Follows dynamic pricing rules (see Section 4).

8.2 Extension Deadline
Requests must be submitted 24h before original checkout time.

9. Maintenance Hold Policies
9.1 Room Reassignment
System autoreassigns guests to an equivalent or upgraded room.
Guest notified via email/SMS with new room details.

9.2 Refund Eligibility
If reassignment impossible: Full refund + 10% discount on future stay.
 
10. Refund Processing
10.1 Refund Timeline
Credit/Debit Cards: 3–5 business days.
Bank Transfers: 5–7 business days.
    
10.2 Refund Method
Refunds issued to original payment method.

11. Policy Enforcement
Automated Enforcement: System applies rules via state transitions (e.g., autocancellation for expired payments).
Manual Override: Hotel staff can override policies for exceptional cases (e.g., medical emergencies).
